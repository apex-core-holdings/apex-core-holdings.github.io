Privacy Notice

Last updated: November 2026

Apex Core Holdings ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This notice explains how we handle the information you provide to us when you contact us through our website.

1. What information we collect

When you email us to request a quote or brief, we collect the information you choose to provide, which typically includes:

  • Your name and business name
  • Your email address
  • Your website or social media links
  • Details about the project you want us to quote on
  • Your budget range

2. How we use your information

We use your information solely to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Prepare a quote and scope of work for you
  • Communicate with you about the project

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. How we protect your information

Your emails are received through Microsoft Outlook. We take reasonable steps to ensure your information is kept secure and accessed only by team members who need it to quote or work on your project.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your email correspondence for as long as necessary to complete your project or to maintain our business records. If you do not proceed with a project, we will delete your information upon request.

5. Your rights (POPIA)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct or delete your information
  • Object to the processing of your information

To exercise any of these rights, please email us at apexcoreos@outlook.com.
